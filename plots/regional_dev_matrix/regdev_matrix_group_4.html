Regional development typology


This page helps you see where your region stands in relation to others across Europe and who shares your development path.

The diagram automatically places your region on a graph where:
Y-axis: Current development status (from low to high)
X-axis: Development trend (from declining to growing)

Your region’s position is accompanied by a short, custom description explaining how it compares to others—whether it’s catching up, keeping pace, or falling behind.

Alongside the diagram, the map of Europe highlights regions with a similar profile and comparable challenges or opportunities. These are your peer regions—places to watch, learn from, and potentially collaborate with.

Together, the graph and map give you a visual overview of how your region is performing, how it’s evolving, and who’s on a similar journey.
