The Resilient Regions Policy Dashboard helps you turn regional development data, migration insights and real policy examples into strategies for rebuilding your region.

Built for regional policymakers, planners, researchers, and changemakers, the dashboard combines novel data and future projections, with human stories to help you:

Identify the strengths and weaknesses in your regional development
See which migration trends your region will face in the near future
Learn from regions that are coping with or reversing brain drain

The Dashboard is built for regional policymakers, planners, researchers, and anyone working to make their region more resilient, attractive, and future-ready.

Policy alone can't reverse every decline trend but it can shape a more resilient future.
