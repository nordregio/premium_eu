Welcome to the PREMIUM_EU Dashboard

A thriving region is no accident – it's the result of smart strategies that attract and keep the people you need.

This Dashboard shows how targeted migration policies can stop regional decline and spark new growth. By combining cutting-edge data with real policy examples, it helps you:

Identify the migrants your region needs – and what draws them in
See how mobility trends shape resilience, innovation, and local economies
Learn from regions that turned brain drain into brain gain
