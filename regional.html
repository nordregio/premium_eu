Explore European Regional Data

This page helps you see where your region stands, understand the forces shaping it, and find policy ideas you can adapt to strengthen its future.

Step 1: Select a Region

Choose a country and region from the dropdowns on the left, or click directly on any region in the interactive map.

Step 2: Explore Data

Once a region is selected, use the tabs above to explore policy solutions, development scores, and migration trends.

What You'll Discover

  • Policy solutions: Tailored to your region’s specific development profile and needs. Learn from examples that have worked in other regions and filter by type of policy.
  • Regional development: See economic, social, and living environment indicators to identify strengths and weaknesses.
  • Migration: Learn about the age, gender, and education of those moving to your region and how these trends will evolve.

Here you can find policy suggestions from your or other regions, based on what has worked to tackle decline and attract newcomers.

These policies should be viewed as examples to inspire adaption and not a one-size-fits-all solution. Almost all policies are linked to regions going through a similar development as your region so you can compare approaches and even reach out to peers.

You can filter policies by relevant policy areas and explore two types of solutions:

  1. 1. AI-scraped and summarised from public sources
  2. 2. Verified by the PREMIUM_EU research team

Disclaimer: AI can make mistakes. Policies come in many formats, from one-page local council decisions to large policy documents. We cannot guarantee that each policy found by AI is relevant to your case or that the summary on screen is completely accurate.
