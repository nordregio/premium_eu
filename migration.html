On this page, you can explore first-hand insights from migrants to better understand the motivations behind moving and moving back.

Filter by economic, social, or living environment factors to see quotes and perspectives describing what influences decisions about work, belonging, housing, and quality of life.

These stories add context to the data shown elsewhere in the Dashboard, helping you see how real experiences shape mobility patterns and what makes regions more or less attractive to newcomers.

This page gives you a map overview of integration across Europe, showing how well different regions support newcomers in settling, working, and participating in society.

Explore regional scores to see where integration is strongest, where challenges remain, and how your region compares. The index is based on in-depth interviews and combines indicators on employment, education, social inclusion, and civic participation to provide a snapshot of how effectively regions turn arrival into belonging.

Use the map to identify leading examples and regions facing similar integration challenges. Insights that can guide more inclusive and resilient regional development.
