Explore integration across Europe: How well different regions support newcomers in settling, working, and participating in society.

What the Integration index shows: Scores range from 0 (low integration) to 1 (high integration) and reflect how well first- and second-generation migrants are settling, working, and participating in society.

How it is calculated: The index combines structural (jobs, housing), cultural (language, values), social (networks, friendships), and political/civic participation indicators to provide a snapshot of how effectively regions turn arrival into belonging.

Navigate the map: Use zoom to identify leading examples and regions facing similar integration challenges.
